# A Thing — Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 26, 2026 ## 1. Introduction A Thing (“the App”) is a personal visual collection application that helps users save, organize, and rediscover things they want to remember. This Privacy Policy explains how A Thing collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App. ## 2. Information We May Process Depending on the features you use and the configuration of the released version, A Thing may process the following information: **Account information:** If you sign in, authentication providers may process information such as your email address, account identifier, and sign-in provider details. **Saved content:** Titles, notes, categories, source links, item statuses, collections, and other information you choose to save. **Images and media:** Images you select, capture, or share with the App, including associated previews and metadata. **Shared links:** URLs and publicly available webpage metadata, such as titles, descriptions, and preview images, when you save links. **AI processing:** If you use AI-assisted features, selected images, text, links, or related item information may be transmitted to the configured AI service to generate suggestions or identify item details. **Technical information:** The App or its service providers may process technical information needed for authentication, synchronization, security, diagnostics, and service operation, depending on the services enabled. ## 3. How We Use Information Information may be used to: - Save and display your personal collection. - Organize, search, and filter saved items. - Provide account authentication and cloud synchronization. - Retrieve link previews and relevant item metadata. - Provide AI-assisted item recognition and suggestions. - Export, restore, and manage your collection. - Diagnose errors, protect the service, and maintain functionality. ## 4. Local Storage and Cloud Services A Thing may store collection data and images locally on your device. If cloud synchronization is enabled, information associated with your collection may be transmitted to configured backend services, which may include Firebase, Google authentication services, and Supabase. The specific information stored by each service depends on the application's configuration. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements. You should not assume that information remains exclusively on your device when cloud or AI features are enabled. ## 5. AI Services Some versions of A Thing may use Google's Gemini AI services to assist with image understanding and item information. When you use an AI feature, relevant content may be sent to the configured AI provider for processing. Do not submit sensitive personal information through AI features unless you understand and accept how that information will be handled. AI-generated suggestions may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed before being saved or relied upon. ## 6. Sharing and Disclosure A Thing does not need a public social feed to provide its core collection features. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide authentication, cloud storage, database operations, link previews, or AI processing. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of users and the service. We do not claim that data is never shared with third parties; service providers may process information as part of the features you use. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion Information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App may not delete information already stored in cloud services. Where account and cloud features are available, you should be able to request or initiate deletion of associated information through the available account controls or by contacting the developer. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or service logs where required for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes. ## 8. Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their devices and accounts protected. ## 9. Children's Privacy A Thing is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed. The intended audience and age eligibility of the published App are governed by its Google Play listing and applicable requirements. ## 10. Your Choices Depending on the features available in your version of A Thing, you may be able to: - Review, edit, or delete saved items. - Choose which images and links to add. - Avoid optional AI features. - Export your collection. - Manage account or synchronization settings. You may also manage relevant app permissions through your Android device settings. ## 11. Third-Party Services The App may rely on third-party services such as: - Google Play services and Google authentication. - Firebase services. - Supabase services. - Google Gemini AI services, when enabled. Their handling of information is also governed by their respective policies and terms. ## 12. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as A Thing evolves.
